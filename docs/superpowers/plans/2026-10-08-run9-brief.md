## 1. Ramp signal and lane (signals.py, arena.py, claude_agents.py)
- `structural_view` adds for bonding-curve coins: `curve_pct` (exists on the token), `curve_pct_chg_10m` (exists in
  chg_since), `ramp` = `"early" (<40) | "ramp" (40-80) | "late" (>80)`, and `ramp_setup: true` when 40 <= curve_pct <= 80,
  `net_share >= 0.1` (or flow missing but buys_h1 > sells_h1), `buyers_1h >= 10`, no veto, and the coin is not in the
  `chart_tell` state below.
- `build_lanes` sorts the `curve` lane: `ramp_setup` coins first (by curve_pct descending), then the rest by the existing
  momentum key. Cap stays 15 plus pins.
- Brief Playbook bullet (first): "Ramp: on the run-8 recordings the only entry that paid was buying a curve coin as its
  fill crossed 40-60% with net buying from many wallets and selling at graduation or a -30% stop; coins that reach
  half the curve graduate about half the time, and the curve's own math lifts the price into graduation. Dip-buys,
  chasing fast fills and buying at first sight all lost after costs. ramp_setup marks coins in that window."
- Arena managed exit: a position opened on a curve coin (`dex == "pumpfun"` at fill) with the order field
  `"sell_at_graduation": true` (optional; the Ramp persona is told to set it) is sold 100% on the tick the token
  shows `pump.graduated` (reason "ramp: graduated, cashing the curve"). Default off.

## 2. Bundle balance check (rugdata.py, signals.py)
- rugdata already calls `getMultipleAccounts` on the top-holder OWNER wallets (rugdata.py ~454). Record each owner's
  `lamports` (SOL balance) into the holders list as `sol` (float SOL). Expose `top5_sol`: the SOL balances of the top 5
  non-LP, non-insider-marked... no: the top 5 holders excluding the pool/LP account and the creator, in holder order.
- `veto_report`: if at least 4 of the 5 have a balance under 0.25 SOL AND the spread (max - min) is under 0.05 SOL,
  veto `"bundle: top holders all carry ~<x> SOL (identical funding)"`. Missing balances → no veto; thresholds via
  `ARENA_VETO_BUNDLE_SOL_MAX=0.25` and `ARENA_VETO_BUNDLE_SOL_SPREAD=0.05`. Soft flag when 3 of 5 match.
- Funding age (video 1 check B) is NOT built (needs 20 signature lookups per token); say so in the docstring.

## 3. Chart tell (arena.py chg_since / signals.py)
- From the arena's 30 s price history: `green_run` = number of consecutive non-falling ticks from the first point;
  `chart_tell: "straight run"` when the coin has at least 8 history points, every tick since tracking began was
  non-falling (allow one -1% wiggle), and price is >= 2x the first point. Shown as a flag `straight green run since
  launch, no pullback (bundle pattern)` in `structure.flags`; NOT a veto (it is a pattern, not proof). Excluded from
  `ramp_setup` (above).

## 4. Hard stop for every Claude position (arena.py check_managed, brief)
- `ARENA_HARD_STOP_PCT=-30`: sell everything when price <= entry * 0.70, unless the position is a moonshot (keeps its
  -50% floor) or the Claude set a tighter stop of its own. Reason "hard stop: -30% from entry". Brief Rules line:
  "Every position has a hard -30% stop from your average entry (moonshots -50%); you may set a tighter one, never a
  looser one. Never average down: a coin under your entry is a coin to leave."
