# Evidence on how profitable Solana / pump.fun memecoin traders actually are (2024-2026)

Scope note for the writer: almost every wallet-level number below comes from Dune Analytics dashboards (principally @adam_tehc, plus @oladee and CoinGecko Research's own Dune queries) reported through crypto press. The dashboards share three limits that must travel with every figure: (1) they measure **realized** PnL only, so wallets still holding tokens that went to zero show as break-even rather than as losers (biases the "share profitable" *up* in bull months) while wallets sitting on unrealized gains show no profit (biases *down*); (2) they count **wallets, not people** — one human or bot can run hundreds of wallets; (3) they do not separate bots from humans. Pump.fun's founder also disputed the early (Jan 2025) figures for excluding post-graduation trades. Figures are labelled by data date.

## Q1. What share of pump.fun / Solana memecoin wallets are profitable, and what does the PnL distribution look like?

### Takeaway
Across every snapshot from Aug 2024 to mid-2025, roughly 60-70% of pump.fun wallets realized a net loss, only ~3-6% realized more than $1,000, ~0.4-0.5% realized more than $10,000, and ~0.002-0.003% (a few hundred wallets) realized more than $1M; the median wallet's realized outcome is a small loss. Monthly series show the profitable share bottoming at ~30% in June 2025 and rising to ~57-73% in Feb-Apr 2026 by one (realized-only, wallet-netted) methodology, but even in that bull window ~96% of wallets ended the month with less than $500 of profit or a loss.

### Cited Findings

**Cumulative / lifetime snapshots (Dune, @adam_tehc):**
- Aug 2024 (data as of ~Aug 19, 2024; ~2.43M user wallets; ~1.8M tokens): "Only 3% of users profit over 1,000 dollars" — top 3.061% of wallets >$1,000 realized profit, top 0.477% >$10,000, top 0.0028% >$1M; dashboard author @adam_tehc, with related Dune work by Hashed, @evelyn233, @jhackworth — [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2139008)
- Aug 2024: "More than two-thirds of wallet addresses on Pump.Fun are unprofitable"; dashboard author's own caveat that the dashboard is "imperfect" because wallet PnL requires assumptions about price and timing — [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/most-users-keep-losing-money-on-pump-fun-despite-the-protocols-record-profits/)
- Jan 10, 2025 (13.55M total pump.fun wallets): 55,296 wallets (0.412%) had realized >$10,000; 0.048% realized >$100,000; ~293 wallets (0.00217%) realized >$1,000,000. Method counts realized PnL only (position closed). Pump.fun co-founder Alon objected that the dashboard excluded purchases made after tokens bonded to Raydium and argued "the true number of profitable... wallets is an order of magnitude LARGER" — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-crypto-traders-majority-do-not-realize-profits-dune-data)

**Six-month window, Dec 2024 - Jun 2025 (Dune, reported June 5, 2025; 4.2M wallets):**
- 62.5% of wallets experienced net realized losses; 27.2% (244,130 wallets) were profitable (the remaining ~10% net to zero, consistent with realized-only accounting where buy-only wallets show $0)
- Profit brackets: <$1k 21.5% of wallets; $1k-$10k 4.5%; $10k-$100k ~1%; $100k-$1M 0.1% (4,705 wallets); >$1M 311 wallets (<0.01%)
- Loss brackets: $0-$1k ~56% (2.4M wallets); $1k-$10k 221,827 wallets; $10k-$100k ~30,000 wallets; >$100k ~1,700 wallets; >$1M 46 wallets — [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/over-60-pump-fun-traders-saw-losses-less-than-0-01-made-over-1m/)
- Same dataset, July 2025 framing (not fetched, 403; per search summary): of addresses that traded more than 10 pump.fun tokens in the prior six months, 56.4% recorded cumulative losses of $0-$1,000, and ~1.3M addresses were active in August — [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/pump-fun-trading-data-majority-lose-money/)

**Monthly series, Apr 2024 - Apr 2026 (CoinGecko Research, Dune, published May 7, 2026; population = monthly active wallets on pump.fun + PumpSwap with any realized buy/sell; realized PnL only, netted at wallet level):**
- Share of wallets profitable by month: Apr 2024 47.58%, May 51.20%, Jun 46.30%, Jul 43.25%, Aug 42.28%, Sep 42.05%, Oct 41.95%, Nov 38.82%, Dec 38.32%; Jan 2025 43.75%, Feb 35.90%, Mar 34.10%, Apr 37.23%, May 36.85%, **Jun 2025 30.08% (low)**, Jul 36.01%, Aug 37.38%, Sep 39.13%, Oct 40.85%, Nov 44.84%, Dec 44.02%; Feb 2026 56.83%, Mar 2026 70.00%, Apr 2026 73.28% — [CoinGecko Research](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback)
- April 2026 magnitude breakdown (~3.15M wallets): profitable $1-$500 = 2.05M wallets (65.1%); $500-$1k = 87K (2.8%); **>$1k = 169K (5.4%)**; losses $1-$500 = 793K (25.2%); $500-$1k = 22K (0.7%); >$1k = 24K (0.8%). Stated caveats: "excludes bagholders who never sold their tokens even if it crashes to zero"; cross-token netting can mask per-token losses; Dune USD prices "can be missing, stale, or distorted for illiquid tokens"; bots and wash trading not filtered — [CoinGecko Research](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback)

**Conflicting March 2026 read (Dune, @oladee, published Mar 24, 2026, trailing month):**
- ~96% of wallets trading pump.fun-launched tokens made less than $500 in the past month; 50.6% posted a loss; 45.6% made $0-$500 profit; two wallets made >$1M and two lost $500k-$1M. Caveat quoted: "the data just shows the number of wallets, and... individual traders can create multiple wallets" — [cryptonews.net](https://cryptonews.net/news/altcoins/32600810/); contradicted on headline direction by [CoinGecko Research](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback), which shows 70.0% of wallets profitable in March 2026. The two use different wallet populations (pump.fun + PumpSwap vs. "wallets trading pump.fun-launched tokens"), different windows (calendar month vs. trailing 30 days to Mar 24) and unspecified differences in PnL netting; neither is a verified primary query.

**Single-token case studies (useful as upper bounds on "hot" launches):**
- TRUMP (Chainalysis, Jan 21, 2025): "over 77% of wallets that hold TRUMP have made losses or earned less than $100"; ~50 whales realized >$10M each; 46.5% of buyers created their wallet the same day they bought; ~50% had never bought a Solana token other than SOL/stables — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/336584/trump-melania-memecoin-holders)
- LIBRA (Nansen, Feb 19, 2025; wallets with |PnL| > $1,000 only): 86% of traders lost, $251M in aggregate losses vs. $180M in profits for the rest; the top winner realized $6.5M from a wallet funded by a Bybit address and a trading-bot user — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/342266/traders-of-solana-based-libra-memecoin-lost-251-million-nansen)

### Inferences
- The "share profitable" is highly regime-dependent (30% in Jun 2025 vs. 73% in Apr 2026 on the same methodology), but the *magnitude* distribution barely moves: the share of wallets clearing >$1k realized profit sat at 3.1% (Aug 2024), ~5.6% (Jun 2025, summing brackets), and 5.4% (Apr 2026). Treat "~1 in 20 wallets ever books >$1k" as the stable fact.
- Because realized-only accounting excludes bagholders, the 2026 bull-month profitable shares (57-73%) are upper bounds; the oladee cut of the same month shows a majority still losing. The honest range for "share of wallets with any realized profit" in 2026 is ~50-73%, versus ~27-45% in 2024-2025.
- The median wallet outcome in every snapshot is a loss under $1,000 (the modal bracket is "lost $0-$1k" in 2025 and "made $1-$500" in Apr 2026).

### Gaps
- No source gives a true median or mean PnL in dollars per wallet, nor a per-*person* distribution (wallet clustering not applied).
- No study reports unrealized PnL or marks held positions to market; the bagholder omission is unquantified.
- The underlying Dune queries were not inspected directly; all figures are press reports of dashboards.

## Q2. How concentrated are profits (top 1% / 0.1%), and how much is bots/insiders vs. humans?

### Takeaway
Profits are extremely concentrated — on the order of 0.1% of wallets book >$100k and ~0.002-0.01% book >$1M, while in single-token events (TRUMP, LIBRA) a few dozen wallets captured most of the gains — and the evidence points to a large share of the top tail being deployer-coordinated snipers, bundlers and bots rather than discretionary humans, though no source puts a clean percentage on "profit share captured by bots."

### Cited Findings
- Top-tail counts: 0.1% of wallets ($100k-$1M, 4,705 wallets) and 311 wallets >$1M out of 4.2M in the Dec 2024-Jun 2025 window — [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/over-60-pump-fun-traders-saw-losses-less-than-0-01-made-over-1m/); 0.048% >$100k and ~293 wallets >$1M of 13.55M lifetime wallets as of Jan 2025 — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-crypto-traders-majority-do-not-realize-profits-dune-data)
- TRUMP: ~50 whale wallets each realized >$10M while >77% of holders lost or made <$100; several wallets held >$100M, representing 94% of total value (Chainalysis) — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/336584/trump-melania-memecoin-holders)
- LIBRA: winners' $180M vs. losers' $251M, top wallet $6.5M, that wallet linked to a trading-bot user (Nansen) — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/342266/traders-of-solana-based-libra-memecoin-lost-251-million-nansen)
- Deployer-funded sniping (Pine Analytics, Apr 21, 2025; window Mar 15-Apr 21, 2025): >15,000 launches sniped in the deployment block by wallets that received SOL directly from the deployer; 4,600+ sniper wallets, 10,400+ deployers; ~1.75% of all pump.fun launches; "over 15,000 SOL in realized net profit" in one month; **87% of snipes were profitable**; typical per-wallet profit 1-100 SOL, some >500 SOL; >55% of snipes fully exited within one minute, ~85% within five minutes, >11% within 15 seconds; >90% involve only one or two swaps; activity clustered 14:00-23:00 UTC. Method identifies only the high-confidence (direct deployer-funded) subset — [Pine Analytics](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/exit-liquidity-machines)
- Manipulation prevalence at scale (Szwajcok, Tsuchiya, Liu, Soska, Payer, Christin, arXiv Sep 9, 2026; all ~15M pump.fun coins Jan 14, 2024-Jan 14, 2026, full history on a 1% random sample = 152,171 coins / 49.97M transactions): conservative wash-trading detection flags 2,221,734 transactions across 8.26% of coins = 66,636 SOL = 1.32% of total volume, rising to 50.31% of coins among those with >10,000 transactions; 4,402 coordinated dumps selling 33,393 SOL total; 1,490,123 copycat coins (10% of all coins) — originals graduate at 9.20% vs. copycats 0.86%; 3,571,170 coins (23.5%) created shortly after a social-media post, and 31 posts each helped their coin's creator earn >= $1M; the top 1% of creator clusters account for 58.57% of all coins; a "Market-Manipulation-as-a-Service" tooling market exists — [arXiv 2609.10246](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10246v1)
- Bot prevalence per project (Solana/pump.fun copy-trading study, arXiv Jan 2026, 6,000 projects via Flipside): "Bundle bots appear in approximately one quarter of meme coin projects" and "Sniper bots appear in the majority of projects" — [arXiv 2601.08641](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08641v2)
- Older bot-volume evidence (Flip Research via Cointelegraph, Jul 30, 2024): Raydium pools with "$3 in liquidity but over $5 million in 24-hour trading volume"; one project with $10.8M volume from $48 liquidity driven by 2,845 bots; "over 50 rug projects that generated $200 million in trading volume"; no overall bot share given — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bots-pushing-solana-over-ethereum-research)
- Soft rug pulls as a profit channel for deployers (Solidus Labs, Feb 2025): 93% (~361,000) of 388,000 Raydium pools examined saw the deployer withdraw >= 90% of liquidity after traders deposited; median incident ~$2,832, 25% under $732, largest $1.9M — [Solidus Labs](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)
- Anecdotal insider-like outcomes: one wallet turned $285 into $627,000 in a day on ZREAL (not fetched; per search summary) — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/crypto-trader-turned-285-627-195313752.html)

### Inferences
- A sniper cohort with an 87% hit rate and sub-minute exits is structurally different from the ~30-45% profitable share of the general wallet population; it is reasonable to infer that a meaningful fraction of the >$10k/>$100k wallets are automated or deployer-coordinated, but no source measures that fraction.
- The Pine subset (1.75% of launches, 15k SOL/month) is a floor on insider extraction, since it only catches wallets funded directly by the deployer.
- Wash trading inflates the apparent "retail interest" in the highest-activity coins (half of coins with >10k txs show wash trades), so volume-based signals used by humans are contaminated by the same actors who profit from them.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies the share of aggregate realized profit captured by labelled bots/insiders vs. non-bot wallets on pump.fun; this is the single biggest missing number.
- A search summary attributed "bots accounted for 62% of Solana's DEX volume in July 2025" to one of [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/solana-dex-volumes-surge-with-over-5-8-m-traders-driving-growth/) / [KuCoin blog](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/es-solana-on-chain-volume-hits-1-6t-in-2025-why-users-are-leaving-cexs); I could not verify the primary source or method, so treat as unconfirmed.
- No Gini coefficient or "top 1% share of profits" is published for pump.fun wallets; only bracket counts.

## Q3. How often does a small (~$1,000) account double or 10x, and what are survival / blow-up rates?

### Takeaway
No source conditions outcomes on starting capital, so there is no direct "$1k account 10x" rate; the best proxies are that ~0.4-0.5% of all wallets ever realize >$10k, ~3-6% realize >$1k, only ~0.5-2% of tokens graduate (and under 1% in early 2025), ~0.005% of tokens ever reach $1M market cap, and in the Dec 2024-Jun 2025 window ~6% of wallets realized losses above $1,000 (the "blew up more than a small account" tail).

### Cited Findings
- Wallet-level upside tail: top 3.061% >$1k, 0.477% >$10k, 0.0028% >$1M (Aug 2024) — [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2139008); 4.5% at $1k-$10k, ~1% at $10k-$100k, 0.1% at $100k-$1M, 311 wallets >$1M of 4.2M (Dec 2024-Jun 2025) — [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/over-60-pump-fun-traders-saw-losses-less-than-0-01-made-over-1m/); 5.4% >$1k in Apr 2026 — [CoinGecko Research](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback)
- Wallet-level downside tail (Dec 2024-Jun 2025): 221,827 wallets lost $1k-$10k, ~30,000 lost $10k-$100k, ~1,700 lost >$100k, 46 lost >$1M, of 4.2M — [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/over-60-pump-fun-traders-saw-losses-less-than-0-01-made-over-1m/); Apr 2026: 0.8% of wallets realized losses >$1k, 0.7% $500-$1k, 25.2% $1-$500 (realized only, bagholders excluded) — [CoinGecko Research](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback)
- Token-level base rates for a "moonshot": 24h graduation rate 1.2% (136 of 11,743) in Aug 2024 — [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/most-users-keep-losing-money-on-pump-fun-despite-the-protocols-record-profits/); 1.5% of 24h launches graduating and 89 tokens of ~2M (0.0045%) above $1M cap, ~0.3% of graduates reaching $1M (Sep 2024) — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/249107/fewer-than-100-pump-fun-tokens-above-1m-market-cap-amid-meme-coin-lull); "the probability of reaching $10M: approximately 1 in 100,000", 96 tokens >$1M and 18 >$10M of ~1.8M (Aug 2024) — [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2139008); of 1.7M tokens only 41 held >$1M cap for "multiple weeks" and 15 held >$10M (Aug 2024) — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/memecoin-casino-bet-pump-odds)
- Graduation trend: best week Nov 2024 at 1.67%; by Feb-Mar 2025 "less than 1%" for four consecutive weeks with weekly graduations averaging ~1,500 — [Cointelegraph via TradingView](https://tr.tradingview.com/news/cointelegraph%3A56f07a8fa094b%3A0-pump-fun-memecoins-are-dying-at-record-rates-less-than-1-survive); Sep 1-Oct 1, 2025: 655,770 tokens, 243,123 creators, 2,600,790 trader addresses, 4,338 graduations = ~0.63%, median ~4.4 minutes and ~457 transactions to graduate, distribution "strongly right-skewed" — [arXiv 2602.14860](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1); Q4 2024 graduation "peaks at less than 2%" (Mancino, arXiv Dec 2025) — [arXiv 2512.11850](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.11850); 2026: "fewer than 0.5% graduate" overall, Aug 2026 18,202 of 1,032,440 launches (1.8%), median time to bond 1 minute, ~30% of graduates bond within 30 minutes and ~80% within 6 hours, "median graduate peak $31K" market cap, with some fast-graduates "5-50x from their launch price within hours" (vendor blog, MadeOnSol Deployer Hunter dataset) — [MadeOnSol](https://madeonsol.com/blog/pump-fun-first-3-minutes-graduation-math)
- Retail composition/onboarding: 46.5% of TRUMP buyers created their wallet the day they bought; ~50% had never bought a Solana altcoin (Chainalysis) — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/336584/trump-melania-memecoin-holders)

### Inferences
- A $1,000 account that 10x's would have to land in the ">$10k realized" bracket; that bracket is ~0.4-0.5% of all wallets over multi-month windows. Since many wallets deploy far more than $1k, the conditional probability for a $1k starter is almost certainly lower than 0.5%, but cannot be computed from published data.
- "Doubling" (>$1k realized on $1k) corresponds roughly to the 3-6% of wallets with >$1k profit; again an upper bound for small starters.
- With a median graduate peaking at ~$31k market cap (2026) and graduation at ~$69k, the typical graduate does not even reach graduation price again for later buyers; "5-50x within hours" applies only to the ~1-2% that graduate fast, and the source is a tooling vendor.
- Blow-up proxy: ~6% of wallets lost >$1k in the 2025 window versus ~5.6% that made >$1k — the tails are roughly symmetric in count, but the loss tail is understated because bagholders are excluded.

### Gaps
- No dataset conditions on initial deposit size; "small account" outcomes are not published anywhere I found.
- A search summary cited "of 15,548 graduates, only 43.8% still had $5k of liquidity 30 minutes later, and 19.7% at 24 hours" and "776,306 tokens tracked, 1.41% graduated, median 4.9 minutes, 83% within an hour" but I could not locate the primary page (not in [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2139008) or [MadeOnSol](https://madeonsol.com/learn/graduated-pump-fun)); treat as unverified.
- No survival-analysis of *wallets* (time until a wallet stops trading, or share of wallets that deposit again after a loss) was found; "churn" claims in press (e.g., a 22% drop in weekly active wallets in late April 2025) could not be traced to a primary source.
- arXiv 2607.02823 ("Auditing collector-generated graduation labels on pump.fun") reportedly documents measurement error and temporal non-generalization in graduation labels; not fetched, but relevant to how much to trust graduation-rate figures.

## Q4. Do returns persist (skill vs. luck)? Does a profitable wallet stay profitable?

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous persistence study for Solana memecoin wallets; the closest evidence is (a) a Solana/pump.fun copy-trading paper showing wallet profitability is partially predictable from past returns and bot indicators (smart-money 14% per trade vs. 3% for a frictions-adjusted copier), (b) structural evidence that the persistently profitable cohort is sniper/insider wallets with 87% hit rates, and (c) BIS evidence from Ethereum DEX liquidity provision that sophisticated participants persistently out-earn retail. The survey-based "skill" claims circulating in press are not on-chain evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Copy-trading study (arXiv 2601.08641v2, Solana/pump.fun, 6,000 projects incl. all creators and traders, Flipside + pump.fun comments): wallet profitability predicted from "historical returns, trade count, statistical significance (t-statistic), and bot activity indicators"; "average return of 14% for identified smart-money trades" vs. "estimated copier return of 3% per trade under realistic market frictions" modelled on the pump.fun bonding curve including the 1% fee; the paper "does not provide statistics on whether profitable wallets maintain profitability over time" — [arXiv 2601.08641](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08641v2)
- Deployer-funded snipers: 87% of snipes profitable with structured, repeated operational patterns across 4,600+ wallets — i.e., a cohort whose edge is informational/positional rather than discretionary — [Pine Analytics](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/exit-liquidity-machines)
- Nansen defines "Smart Money" by win rate, ROI thresholds, portfolio diversity and "consistency (performance through market cycles)" and sells a "consistent winners" leaderboard; this is a commercial labelling product, not a published persistence study — [Nansen Academy](https://academy.nansen.ai/en/articles/9672038-finding-consistent-winners-with-smart-money-leaderboard)
- Adjacent evidence (Ethereum DEX liquidity provision, not memecoin trading): BIS Working Paper 1227 finds liquidity provision concentrated in a small sophisticated group that earns significantly higher profits than retail and exhibits "considerable skill", especially in high volatility — [BIS](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1227.htm)
- Press/survey claims of skill (e.g., "investors who take memecoins seriously achieve a 74% profit rate" vs. 21% for gamblers) come from a 1,000-respondent self-report survey, not on-chain data — [BitPinas](https://bitpinas.com/feature/memecoins-only-profitable-asset/) (not fetched; per search summary)

### Inferences
- The predictability of wallet PnL from past t-stats and bot flags (copy-trading paper) implies *some* persistence, but the fact that the predictive features include bot indicators suggests the persistent edge is largely automation/insider access, not human skill.
- The 14% -> 3% gap between smart-money and copier returns is a direct estimate of how much of observed "skill" is execution timing that cannot be replicated by a follower paying fees and slippage.

### Gaps
- No paper or dashboard tracks the same pump.fun wallets across consecutive periods and reports transition probabilities (profitable -> profitable). This is a genuine gap in the literature as of the sources found.
- A search summary attributed to a Nansen research piece that "the overall median win rate for all active Ethereum DEX traders dropped to a record low of 26.29% in the twelve months ending August 2026" with "robust evidence of cross-sectional persistence"; the URL redirected to Nansen's blog index and I could not verify it.
- An AFT 2026 paper ([LIPIcs.AFT.2026.36](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol395-aft2026/LIPIcs.AFT.2026.36/LIPIcs.AFT.2026.36.pdf)) surfaced in a persistence search but its PDF could not be parsed; title and findings unknown.

## Q5. What is the expected value of buying random new launches or graduated tokens, after fees?

### Takeaway
Every quantitative source implies negative expected value for an uninformed buyer: 98.6% of pump.fun tokens with >= 5 trades collapse below $1k liquidity, 93% of Raydium pools get soft-rugged, well under 2% of launches graduate (0.63% in Sep 2025), a formal model finds buy-and-hold on the bonding curve "lies below the breakeven curve," and round-trip costs (1% bonding-curve fee each way, 0.3% on PumpSwap, plus priority fees/Jito tips and bot-frontend fees) consume a few percent per round trip before slippage.

### Cited Findings
- Token collapse rates (Solidus Labs, Feb 2025): >7M pump.fun tokens with >= 5 trades deployed Jan 2024-Mar 2025; only ~97,000 kept liquidity above $1,000; "98.6% of tokens on Pump.fun collapse into worthless pump-and-dump schemes shortly after launch" (method: tracked SOL remaining in the curve after each buy/sell; tokens that migrated to Raydium excluded); 93% of 388,000 Raydium pools soft-rugged (deployer withdrew >= 90% of liquidity), median incident ~$2,832; the report gives no aggregate investor-loss total — [Solidus Labs](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)
- Breakeven analysis (Marino et al., arXiv Feb 2026): graduation probability conditioned on virtual SOL reserves alone "lies below the breakeven curve," so "it is not possible to make profits with a buy-and-hold strategy based only on vSol" under their simplified model; graduation rate ~0.63% (Sep 2025) — [arXiv 2602.14860](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1)
- Worked odds (Aug 2024, @adam_tehc): of 16,357 tokens launched in 24h, 175 graduated and only 19 were "above the threshold" — "from launch to profitable graduation that's 0.12%" (1 in 833); a $100 buy at $5,000 market cap exiting at the $69,000 graduation cap returns $1,380 — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/memecoin-casino-bet-pump-odds)
- Graduation does not imply survival: "Many tokens that have successfully graduated have not performed well... with their market values nearing zero" and only 96 of ~1.8M tokens ever exceeded $1M cap (Aug 2024) — [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2139008); ~0.3% of graduates reach $1M (Sep 2024) — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/249107/fewer-than-100-pump-fun-tokens-above-1m-market-cap-amid-meme-coin-lull); 2026 "median graduate peak $31K" market cap (below the ~$69k graduation cap) — [MadeOnSol](https://madeonsol.com/blog/pump-fun-first-3-minutes-graduation-math)
- Copycats graduate at 0.86% vs. originals 9.20%, and 10% of all coins are copycats — [arXiv 2609.10246](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10246v1)
- Fee schedule: pump.fun charges 1% of each buy or sell on the bonding curve; PumpSwap trades 0.30% (0.20% LP + 0.05% protocol + 0.05% creator, creator share applying to coins since May 13, 2025); graduation fee 0.015 SOL; coin creation 0 SOL — [MadeOnSol](https://madeonsol.com/blog/pumpfun-revenue-sharing-pumpswap-fees-creators); "Project Ascend" dynamic creator fees scale from 0.95% for tokens under $300k market cap down to 0.05% above $20M, replacing the flat 0.05% model introduced in May 2025 — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/pumpdotfun-fee-model)
- Execution costs on top: typical priority fee + Jito tip ~0.0003 SOL (quiet) to ~0.005 SOL (busy) to ~0.05 SOL (congested) per transaction — [MadeOnSol](https://madeonsol.com/blog/solana-priority-fees-guide); BullX default priority tip 0.01 SOL with a 0.002 SOL minimum MEV bribe — [Publish0x](https://www.publish0x.com/solana-trading-bots/bullx-fees-explained-xywpdkg); "a small set of MEV-driven actors are responsible for the majority of tip volume in PvP environments like Pump.fun" — [Pine Analytics](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/jitos-role-in-solana-deep-dive) (not fetched; per search summary)
- Copier friction estimate: following profitable wallets yields ~3% per trade vs. 14% for the originator after fees/slippage/latency — [arXiv 2601.08641](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08641v2)
- Protocol-side extraction for context: pump.fun earned ~$94.5M cumulative fees by Aug 2024 ($6.3M in one week) while >2/3 of wallets lost — [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/most-users-keep-losing-money-on-pump-fun-despite-the-protocols-record-profits/)

### Inferences
- Rough EV for a random launch buy using the Aug 2024 numbers: 0.12% chance of ~13.8x (hold to graduation) = ~1.7% of stake from the winners, against ~99.9% of launches where the buyer is likely selling into a collapsing curve; even generous assumptions about partial recoveries on the losers leave EV well below 1.0x. The 2025-2026 graduation rates (0.5-0.8% in weak months) make this worse, not better.
- Round-trip fixed cost for a bonding-curve trade is ~2% (1% in, 1% out) plus 0.0006-0.1 SOL in priority/tips plus any bot-frontend fee; on a $100 position in a congested period the tips alone can approach 10% of the stake. For graduated tokens on PumpSwap the fee is ~0.6% round trip plus the dynamic creator fee (up to 0.95% per side for sub-$300k caps), so small-cap graduates cost ~2.5% per round trip.
- "Graduated" is a volume filter, not a quality filter: with the median 2026 graduate peaking below its graduation cap, a buyer at or after graduation is more likely than not to be above the token's eventual peak.

### Gaps
- No source computes a full after-fee expected return per trade for a random buyer with realistic exit behavior; the figures above are assembled from separate pieces.
- I could not verify the commonly cited ~1% per-trade fee charged by bot frontends (Photon, BullX, Axiom, Trojan) from a primary page; only BullX's priority-tip defaults were sourced.
- No source gives post-graduation price-path statistics (share of graduates that trade below graduation price after 1h/24h/7d) from a verifiable primary dataset; the "19.7% retain $5k liquidity at 24h" figure is unverified (see Q3 gaps).
- Sources disagree on headline pump.fun fee levels over time (1% flat in 2024; creator-fee changes in May and Sep 2025); a precise fee timeline per period was not assembled.
